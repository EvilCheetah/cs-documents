\subsection{Success Metrics}
Project success will be measured with the quantifiable measurements outlined in
the business objectives, which include:

\begin{itemize}
    \item The centralization of ALL event planning and communication into a
    single application.

    \item A notable numerical percentage increase in event attendance from past
    events.

    \item A numerical percentage decrease in the mentors' time spent manually
    planning.
\end{itemize}

The biggest factor in achieving success will be the centralization of event
coordination. Meeting this key metric will help lead to the other success
metrics being met.
