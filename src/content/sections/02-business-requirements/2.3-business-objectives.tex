\subsection{Business Objectives}
The primary business objectives of this product are to improve operational
efficiency, increase scholar engagement, and reduce manual administrative work
for mentors. The implementation of this product should centralize 100\% of event
planning and communication into a single application and increase event
attendance. The implementation of this product will benefit {\ClientName} by
streamlining event management, making the experience seamless for event
attendees, saving mentors time, and, in turn, boosting event attendance.
