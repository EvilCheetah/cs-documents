\subsection{Product Vision Statement}
The {\ClientName} Schedule Planner will be a dedicated web application that
streamlines the creation and organization of events run by the program. It will
lessen the burden on mentors, allowing them to easily create events shared only
with the relevant parties and accurately track who plans to attend. The planner
will also assist mentees in keeping track of their schedules, providing a
specialized view of their event calendar and helping them learn of new events.
This product will replace the current manual email and text communication
system, which is inefficient and tedious.
