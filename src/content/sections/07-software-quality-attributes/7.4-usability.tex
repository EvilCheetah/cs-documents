\subsection{Usability}

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{USAB-1:} RSVP'd and eligible events shall have different
    appearances on a mentee's personalized calendar for clear distinction.

    \item \textbf{USAB-2:} Mentor can perform event creation, deletion, editing,
    invitations, and time-conflict handling from their mentor dashboard with at
    most 5 button clicks (excluding input field clicks).
    
    \item \textbf{USAB-3:} Mentees can RSVP/un-RSVP on their personalized
    Calendar with at most 3 button clicks (excluding input field clicks).
\end{itemize}
