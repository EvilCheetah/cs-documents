\subsection{Assumptions and Dependencies}

Assumed factors that could affect the requirements stated in RD include
interactions among the different roles. Should the super admin account also be a
normal account? Can mentors also invite and view other mentor events? How much
information should a mentee be able to see on the event page? The project
depends on Microsoft Outlook calendars and Google calendars. Also, it is assumed
that mentors and Mentees have a stable internet connection. Time conflicts are
assumed to occur when an event overlaps with another event.
