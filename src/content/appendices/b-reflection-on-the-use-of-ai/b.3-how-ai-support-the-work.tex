\subsection{How AI Support the Work}

AI tools were used selectively to accelerate specific tasks rather than to
generate the document as a whole. ChatGPT was used to produce an initial list of
terms for the glossary and to clarify technical concepts such as role-based
access control, the Microsoft Graph API, and traceability practices. Gemini was
used as a research tool to help explore permission structures. In all cases, AI
output served as a reference rather than a final product.
