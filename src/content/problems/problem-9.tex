\item[\textbf{Problem 9}]
Explain the feasibility and security of RSA.

\Solution

\subsection{Feasibility}

The \textbf{feasibility} of RSA relies on the fact that computers can easily and
quickly multiply two very large prime numbers together to generate cryptographic
keys, making encryption and decryption fast enough for practical use. The
\textbf{security} of RSA relies on the fact that reversing this process,
factoring that massive resulting number back into its original primes, is
computationally infeasible. Without the private key, it would take modern
computers an impossibly long time to crack the encryption.
