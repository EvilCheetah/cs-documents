\item[\textbf{Problem 2}]
Describe the differences between digital signature and digital certificate.

\Solution

A digital signature is a cryptographic mechanism used to verify the authenticity
and integrity of a message, document, or piece of data.

A digital certificate is a digital document (structured file) issued by a
trusted third party called a Certificate Authority (CA). Its purpose is to bind
a public key to an identity (a person, organization, or device).

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{l | C | C}
Feature      & Digital Signature                            & Digital Certificate                       \\\hline
\hline
Nature       & Cryptographic operation or output            & Structured data file or document          \\\hline
Purpose      & Proves authenticity and integrity of data    & Binds a public key to a specific identity \\\hline
Created by   & The message sender (using their private key) & A trusted Certificate Authority (CA)      \\\hline
Protects     & A specific message or document               & A public key and its owner's identity     \\\hline
Trust anchor & Mathematical proof (asymmetric cryptography) & A trusted third party (the CA)            \\
\end{tabularx}
