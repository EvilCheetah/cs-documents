\item[\textbf{Problem 11}]
What is the TCP/IP protocol? Specify what is the purpose of this protocol, and
what kind of information it provides.

\Solution

The Internet Protocol (IP) is the address system of the Internet and has the
core function of delivering packets of information from a source device to a
target device. IP is the primary way in which network connections are made, and
it establishes the basis of the Internet. IP does not handle packet ordering or
error checking. Such functionality requires another protocol, often the
Transmission Control Protocol (TCP)~\cite{cloudflare_tcp_ip}.

Together, the TCP/IP protocol provides critical routing and delivery
information, including source and destination IP addresses, port numbers to
identify specific applications, and sequence numbers to reassemble data
correctly.
