\item[\textbf{Problem 3}]
\textbf{Add} the following points on the graph you just drew and \textbf{label}
the class of each according to the AN.
\begin{enumerate}
    \item $(1.0; 0.2)$
    \item $(0.0; 0.0)$
    \item $(-0.1; -0.5)$
    \item $(1.7; 0.1)$
    \item $(1.8; -1.4)$
\end{enumerate}

\Solution

Before adding the points to the graph, let's identify the class of each point.
Our equation is $net = 0.5 + x_{1} - 0.3x_{2}$.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Point $(1.0; 0.2)$
          \begin{alignat*}{2}
            \Rightarrow & \quad & 0.5 + 1.0 - 0.3 \cdot 0.2 &= 0.5 + 1.0 - 0.06 \\
            \Rightarrow & \quad &                           &= 1.44
          \end{alignat*}
          Since $\textit{net} = 1.44 \ge 0$, the
          \textbf{point $\mathbf{(1.0; 0.2)}$ is classified as Class 1}.

    \item Point $(0.0; 0.0)$ \\
          From Problem 2, we already found that the
          \textbf{point $\mathbf{(0.0; 0.0)}$ is classified as Class 1}.

    \item Point $(-0.1; -0.5)$
          \begin{alignat*}{2}
            \Rightarrow & \quad & 0.5 - 0.1 - 0.3 \cdot (-0.5) &= 0.5 - 0.1 + 0.15 \\
            \Rightarrow & \quad &                              &= 0.55
          \end{alignat*}
          Since $\textit{net} = 0.55 \ge 0$, the
          \textbf{point $\mathbf{(-0.1; -0.5)}$ is classified as Class 1}.

    \item Point $(1.7; 0.1)$
          \begin{alignat*}{2}
            \Rightarrow & \quad & 0.5 + 1.7 - 0.3 \cdot 0.1 &= 0.5 + 1.7 - 0.03 \\
            \Rightarrow & \quad &                           &= 2.17
          \end{alignat*}
          Since $\textit{net} = 2.17 \ge 0$, the
          \textbf{point $\mathbf{(1.7; 0.1)}$ is classified as Class 1}.

    \item Point $(1.8; -1.4)$
          \begin{alignat*}{2}
            \Rightarrow & \quad & 0.5 + 1.8 - 0.3 \cdot (-1.4) &= 0.5 + 1.8 + 0.42 \\
            \Rightarrow & \quad &                              &= 2.72
          \end{alignat*}
          Since $\textit{net} = 2.72 \ge 0$, the
          \textbf{point $\mathbf{(1.8; -1.4)}$ is classified as Class 1}.
\end{enumerate}
