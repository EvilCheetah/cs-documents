\item[\textbf{Problem 2}]
\textbf{Draw} (on graph or engineering paper or by using software) the decision
boundary encoded by this AN. Be sure to indicate the ``font'' (Class 1) side of
the boundary.

\Solution

To identify the slope, we start from the general form of an
$N$-dimensional hyperplane:

\begin{alignat*}{2}
                & \quad & w_{0}x_{0} + w_{1}x_{1} + w_{2}x_{2}               &= 0                                   \\
    \Rightarrow & \quad & 0.5 \cdot 1 + 1.0 \cdot x_{1} + (-0.3) \cdot x_{2} &= 0                                   \\
    \Rightarrow & \quad & 0.5 + x_{1} - 0.3x_{2}                             &= 0                                   \\
    \Rightarrow & \quad & 0.5 + x_{1}                                        &= 0.3x_{2}                            \\
    \Rightarrow & \quad & x_{2}                                              &= \frac{x_{1}}{0.3} + \frac{0.5}{0.3} \\
\end{alignat*}

This can be plotted as follows:

\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.8\linewidth]{problem-2-graph-without-class.pdf}
    \captionof{figure}{Decision Boundary for the Artificial Neural Unit}
    \label{figure:slope-for-an-unit}
\end{minipage}

To identify the classification side, we choose a test point on one side of the
boundary and evaluate the \emph{net} (step) function. For example, take $(0,0)$
as a random test point. Substituting into the hyperplane equation gives:

\begin{alignat*}{2}
                & \quad & 0.5 + x_{1} - 0.3x_{2} &= 0.5 + 0 - 0.3 \cdot 0 \\
    \Rightarrow & \quad &                        &= 0.5                   \\
\end{alignat*}

Since $\textit{net} = 0.5 \ge 0$, the point $(0,0)$ is classified as Class 1.
Therefore, the Class 1 (``font'') side of the decision boundary is the half-plane
where
\[
0.5 + x_{1} - 0.3x_{2} \ge 0
\quad \Longleftrightarrow \quad
x_{2} \le \frac{x_{1}}{0.3} + \frac{0.5}{0.3}.
\]

    \captionof{figure}{Decision boundary with the Class 1 side indicated}
    \label{figure:slope-for-an-unit-with-classification}
\end{minipage}
