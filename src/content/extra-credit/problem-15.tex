\item[\textbf{Problem 15}]
The expanded Needham-Schroeder Protocol can be shortened to a 6-message protocol
without loss of security by merely removing the 7th message. Why is this true?   \\
(Hint: the purpose of the 7th message is to prove to Bob that he is talking to
Alice, but he already knows that. Why?)                                          \\
\textcolor{red}{(10 extra credit points for both graduates and undergraduates)}

\Solution

In the Expanded Needham-Schroeder Protocol, the seventh message, intended for
Bob to prove his active communication with Alice, is entirely redundant. Bob has
already authenticated Alice in the sixth message by using his secret key to
decrypt her ticket and reveal her name. Because this successfully confirms her
identity to Bob, the seventh message can be safely removed from the protocol
without compromising its security.
